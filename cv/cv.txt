SELECTED WORK
-------------

Evidence-graded gene curation from the literature
2025 – present

MSc data-science thesis · with the TomsBioLab research group

Pipeline that turns grapevine-genomics papers into evidence-graded catalogue rows: language models read, deterministic code makes every call that has to be reproducible.
The model never assigns the evidence level. It answers a fixed set of yes/no questions and tested code maps those booleans onto the published 6-level scale — so a level can be re-derived, audited and unit-tested, which an opinion cannot.
98.7 % level accuracy against a hand-curated gold set — 74 of 75 genes across 9 scorable papers. Providers sit behind one interface, so a lab can run the whole pipeline on its own model.
Findings are held for the January defence; the engineering is documented in the portfolio.

High-throughput screen for heat-stress protection in pollen
2024

MSc plant-breeding thesis (COMAV, UPV) · graded 10/10

Screened 800 compounds for protection of rapeseed (Brassica napus DH4079) pollen against a heat shock that collapses germination from ≈61 % to below 2 %; returned 8 candidate hits as a shortlist for follow-up.
Designed the assay, ran the statistics behind the results, and reported the negative controls — DMSO protects at mild heat (32 °C) but not at 38 °C.
